# Background reading: Barak's *Introduction to Theoretical Computer Science*

Boaz Barak's [*Introduction to Theoretical Computer Science*](https://introtcs.org/public/) is a free online textbook (also available as a [single PDF](https://introtcs.org/public/introtcs.pdf)) that covers several of our topics from the computability side. The table below maps the blocks of our course to the corresponding chapters and sections, for use as optional background reading.

Topics marked *no direct counterpart* are not covered in the book; the pointers given are the nearest related material.

A note on perspective: Barak's book approaches programming languages via *models of computation* (circuits, Turing machines, λ calculus), whereas our course approaches computability via *programming languages* (rewriting, interpreters, proofs). Where the two overlap — λ calculus, Church encodings, the Y combinator, universal evaluators — the book makes a good second source with full proofs.
